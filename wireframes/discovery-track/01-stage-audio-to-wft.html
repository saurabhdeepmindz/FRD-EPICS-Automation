The stakeholder is requesting an extension to the existing BA Tool that adds a Discovery & Solutioning track upstream of the current FRD-to-FTC flow. The new track ingests audio recordings of client requirements and produces a Business Requirements Document, an Approach Note (versioned), lo-fi wireframes, and hi-fi mockups — all of which can subsequently feed into EPIC generation as additional context.

The implementation must reuse the existing BA Tool primitives (project model, storage layer, AI service, artifact viewer) without modifying the existing 8-step module flow. PRD generation should optionally accept Approach-Note-derived context...
